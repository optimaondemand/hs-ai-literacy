AI as Tutor, Collaborator, and Temptation — Individual Submission

Submit in CanvasIndividual work

Purpose: Personal AI-use principles for schoolwork, including a clear disclosure rule and three concrete examples.
Learning target: Apply academic-integrity principles to individual AI-use scenarios.

What to submit

  1. Personal AI-use principles for schoolwork, including a clear disclosure rule and three concrete examples.
  2. A short reflection answering: What makes an AI use supportive rather than substitutive in one scenario?
  3. An AI-use disclosure using the form below.

Directions

  1. Complete the lesson’s interactive or its accessible text fallback.
  2. Complete the required work in your own document or Canvas text-entry box.
  3. Use clear headings, quoted evidence or links where relevant, and complete sentences.
  4. Submit one file or text entry before the Canvas due date.

AI-use disclosure (required)

This is a student-authored policy. You may brainstorm privately with AI, but you must disclose that use and revise the final policy in your own words.

At the end of your submission, include:

  • Tool(s) used: none, or name each approved tool.
  • Purpose: brainstorming, search-term generation, organization, feedback, testing, or another specific use.
  • What you accepted, revised, or rejected: explain your decision.
  • Verification: identify the sources or checks you used for factual claims.

Success criteria

  • Principles are specific, workable, aligned with truthful authorship, and include a disclosure rule plus examples of allowed, limited, and prohibited use.
  • Writing is organized, specific, and understandable to a reader who did not complete the activity.
  • Evidence and uncertainty are represented truthfully.
  • AI use, if any, is disclosed accurately and the student remains responsible for the final submission.
