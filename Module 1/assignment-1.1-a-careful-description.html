AI Literacy • Module 1 • Assignment 1.1

A Careful Description

Describe one everyday system without relying on “smart” as an explanation.

Estimated time15–20 minutes
Points10
Submission typeCanvas text entry
Word count150–200 words
Assignment question: How can you describe an everyday system accurately without assuming it understands, learns, or makes wise decisions?

Your task

Choose one system you encounter in daily life. It may come from school, home, entertainment, transportation, shopping, sports, health/fitness, or creative work. Examples include a navigation app, music recommender, phone camera feature, video feed, spam filter, autocorrect, online store, thermostat, or chatbot.

Write a 150–200 word description using the four-question system check from Lesson 1.1. Your goal is not to prove exactly how the system is built. Your goal is to separate what you can observe from what you reasonably infer.

Your response must include all five parts

  1. Name and context: What is the system, and where do you encounter it?
  2. Inputs: What information does it likely receive?
  3. Outputs: What does it produce, recommend, label, predict, or do?
  4. Process and change: What process seems to connect the input and output? Does it appear to adapt or change over time? Clearly label uncertainty or inference.
  5. Responsible limit: End with one claim the system should not make about itself—for example, a claim that it understands, knows, decides fairly, or is always correct.

Model of careful language

Avoid vague claimsPrefer observable, qualified claims
“My music app knows me.”“My music app appears to rank songs using my listening history and patterns from other users. It may predict what I will enjoy, but it does not know all of my reasons for listening.”
“The chatbot understands history.”“The chatbot generates historical-sounding answers from patterns in language. A fluent answer does not guarantee that its statements are accurate or that it understands the events it describes.”
“The map decides the best route.”“The map app compares possible routes using location, map, and traffic information. Its recommendation may be useful, but ‘best’ depends on goals and circumstances the app may not know.”

Evidence and uncertainty

You are allowed to make reasonable inferences. Use phrases such as appears to, likely, may use, based on the information available, or I cannot verify whether. If you look up a factual detail about the system, name or link the source at the end of your response.

AI-use policy for this assignment

Do not use generative AI to write or rewrite your response. This assignment measures your own ability to make a careful system description. You may use a reliable non-AI source to confirm a factual detail, but you must write in your own words and identify the source.

At the bottom of your submission, add one disclosure line:

  • No AI assistance used. or
  • AI assistance used: [tool] helped me with [specific limited purpose].

Success criteria

  • I named one specific system and its context.
  • I identified plausible inputs and outputs.
  • I described the process cautiously, separating observation from inference.
  • I addressed whether the system changes or adapts.
  • I stated one honest limitation or overclaim the system should not make.
  • I stayed within 150–200 words and included my AI-use disclosure.
