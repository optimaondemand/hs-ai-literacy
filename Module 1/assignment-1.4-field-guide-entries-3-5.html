Field Guide Entries 3–5

Complete three system X-rays using evidence, cautious language, and meaningful limitations.

Assignment question: What does each selected system do, what shapes its behavior, and what should a user not assume?

Your task

Complete entries 3–5 of your Module 1 AI Field Guide. Use three different systems from your candidate list. You may write directly in Canvas, upload a document, or use a plain-text table. Each entry must be understandable on its own.

Required fields for each entry

  1. System name and context: where people encounter it.
  2. Precise task name: classification, prediction, recommendation, generation, perception, planning/control, or a combination.
  3. Likely inputs: information the system receives.
  4. Outputs: labels, rankings, suggestions, content, or actions it produces.
  5. Likely process or data use: distinguish direct evidence from inference.
  6. Human choices: name at least one choice about goals, data, rules, access, or oversight.
  7. Benefit: one useful purpose under appropriate conditions.
  8. Limitation, risk, or unanswered question: one meaningful boundary.
  9. Evidence: a source/citation or a clearly labeled inference.

AI-use policy

You may use reputable sources to verify factual details. Clearly label what you infer from observable behavior. Do not use generative AI to draft or rewrite your entries. Add one disclosure line at the bottom of the submission:

  • No AI assistance used. or
  • AI assistance used: [tool] helped me with [specific limited purpose].

Success criteria

  • Each entry identifies a task precisely.
  • Inputs, outputs, and human choices are specific.
  • Benefits and limitations are stated with appropriate caution.
  • Claims are supported by evidence or labeled as inferences.
  • All three entries include the required fields and disclosure.
