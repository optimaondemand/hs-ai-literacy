Concept Map: Names, Categories, and Claims About AI

Show how five important terms relate without treating them as synonyms.

❦
Estimated time15–20 minutes
Points10
Submission typeCanvas file upload or text entry
FormatMap, diagram, or annotated table

Assignment question: How do computer science, algorithms, automation, AI, and machine learning overlap—and how are they different?

Your task

Create a concept map, diagram, or annotated table that explains the relationships among computer science, algorithm, automation, artificial intelligence (AI), and machine learning (ML). You may draw it on paper and upload a clear photo, use a Canvas text entry/table, or create a digital document. Your work must be readable without an outside account.

Your submission must include

  1. A definition in your own words for each of the five terms.
  2. At least two accurate overlap relationships (for example, ML is an approach within AI; algorithms appear throughout computing).
  3. One example that fits multiple labels, with an explanation.
  4. One example that is automation but not ML, with an explanation.
  5. One revision of a vague claim such as “Everything automated is AI” or “AI and ML mean the same thing.”

Accessible text-only option: Use a five-row table with columns: term, my definition, relationship to another term, example, and evidence/reason. Then add a short paragraph explaining two overlaps and one important difference.

Evidence and careful language

Use phrases such as the available description supports, appears to, likely, or not enough information is provided when needed. Do not assume a system uses ML merely because it is automated or marketed as AI.

AI-use policy

Do not use generative AI to create the map, diagram, or explanations. This assignment measures your own command of the vocabulary. You may use a reliable non-AI source to verify a factual detail; name or link it if you do. Add one disclosure line at the bottom:

  • No AI assistance used. or
  • AI assistance used: [tool] helped me with [specific limited purpose].
