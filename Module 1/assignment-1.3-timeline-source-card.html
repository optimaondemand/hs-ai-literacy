Use a credible source to distinguish a historical promise from demonstrated capability.

20–25 minutes

Submission type

200–250 words

Assignment question: What did one important AI milestone promise, what could it actually do, and what limitation or consequence followed?

Your task

Choose one milestone from Lesson 1.3: formal logic/computation; Ada Lovelace or Alan Turing; Dartmouth and symbolic AI; expert systems; AI winters; machine learning/deep learning; or generative AI. Write a 200–250 word source card that explains the milestone accurately and cautiously.

Your response must include

  1. Milestone and date/context: identify the development clearly.
  2. Hope or promise: explain what people hoped it would address.
  3. Demonstrated capability: state what it could actually do at the time.
  4. Limitation or consequence: identify one boundary, disappointment, or effect.
  5. Credible source: name and link/cite one reliable source used to verify your description.
Source guidance: Prefer a university, museum, library, professional organization, scholarly encyclopedia, or reputable history of computing source. Do not use an unverified social-media post as your only evidence.

AI-use policy

Do not use generative AI to draft or rewrite this source card. You may use non-AI sources for research and must cite at least one. Add one disclosure line at the bottom:

  • No AI assistance used. or
  • AI assistance used: [tool] helped me with [specific limited purpose].

Success criteria

  • I clearly distinguish hope from demonstrated capability.
  • I name a meaningful limitation or consequence.
  • I use a credible cited source.
  • I write in my own words and meet the word count.
  • I include an AI-use disclosure.
