Today’s Focus Question: What does fluent AI language output establish—and what does it not establish?

What you will be able to do

  • Distinguish following symbol rules from understanding a language.
  • Summarize the Chinese Room thought experiment.
  • State one serious objection or alternative interpretation.
  • Make a qualified claim about fluent AI language output.

What you need: a way to record symbols and write a 350–500 word Canvas response. No outside account is required.

  2. 2

    Read and connect

    12 minutes

    Read Reading 1.5. In your notes, define syntax, semantics, and behavioral performance. Then write one sentence connecting your rule-following experience to Searle’s distinction.

    Reasoning check: The Chinese Room does not prove that AI outputs are always wrong or useless. It asks whether correct symbol manipulation alone establishes understanding of meaning.

  3. 3

    Consider a serious objection

    8 minutes

    Explain the systems reply in your own words: perhaps the whole system—not the person alone—could understand. You may also consider whether embodiment, learning, or causal connection to the world changes the question.

  4. 4

    Guided Practice 1.5

    8–10 minutes

    Complete the Canvas quiz. You may retake it; feedback is part of the lesson.

    • Identify what the person in the room does.
    • Choose Searle’s central challenge.
    • Explain the systems reply.
    • Identify why “AI outputs are always wrong” does not follow.
    • Distinguish syntax from semantics using an example.
  5. 5

    Independent application: Chinese Room Reflection

    20–25 minutes

    Open Assignment 1.5 — Chinese Room Reflection. Explain your experience, Searle’s distinction, one serious objection, and your provisional conclusion.

    AI-use guidance: Do not use AI to generate, rewrite, or edit this reflection. It assesses your individual reasoning.

    Interactive · Qualified claim builderBefore you write

    Sort each phrase from Reading 1.5 into the column where it belongs. The sentence underneath assembles a qualified claim you can carry into your reflection.

    Fluent output establishes…

    …but does not, by itself, settle

    Fluent output establishes an observable capability, but does not, by itself, settle every question about meaning, truth, experience, or responsibility.
    Seven phrases. Place each one.

  6. 6

    Build your AI Field Guide

    5 minutes

    Choose one language-based or conversational system. Add a 100–150 word note describing its observable capabilities and one conclusion a user should not draw merely from fluent output.
