Model Experiment Plan

Plan a low-risk, inspectable model investigation before training or simulating a model.

Estimated time
20–25 minutes
Points
15
Submission
Canvas text entry or document
Required
At least 12 proposed test cases

Task: Draft a plan for a small no-code classifier or teacher-provided simulated model. Use non-sensitive, teacher-approved or self-created examples. The goal is investigation, not the highest score.

Required plan sections

  1. Purpose and bounded task.
  2. Categories/possible outputs.
  3. Training examples you could use or simulate; explain why they are appropriate.
  4. Success criteria beyond one percentage.
  5. At least 12 proposed test cases, including normal, ambiguous, and likely difficult cases.
  6. One false-positive concern and one false-negative concern.
  7. One condition that might cause distribution shift.
  8. Human oversight/appropriate-use boundary.
Safety boundary: Do not use private student records, faces, voices, health, discipline, admissions, legal, financial, or other sensitive/high-stakes data. Use teacher-provided, public-domain, self-created, or simulated examples.

AI-use disclosure

Do not use generative AI to draft the plan. You may use course materials and teacher-provided resources. Add a disclosure line.

Success criteria

  • Task and categories are narrow and appropriate.
  • Test cases show deliberate variation.
  • Error costs and limits are meaningful.
  • Safety and oversight are clear.
