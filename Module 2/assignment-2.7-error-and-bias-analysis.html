Error and Bias Analysis

Trace an error pattern to plausible upstream causes, then propose a test, mitigation, or appropriate-use boundary.

Estimated time
20–25 minutes
Points
12
Submission
Canvas text entry
Length
300–400 words or structured table

Task: Use your lab/simulation results or the fictional dataset from Lesson 2.7. Analyze one error pattern. You are not required to prove a single cause; you are required to reason from evidence and uncertainty.

Required elements

  1. Describe the error pattern or observation.
  2. Name at least two plausible upstream causes from sampling, labeling, measurement, proxy choice, or missing data.
  3. Explain the evidence that supports each possibility and what remains unknown.
  4. Propose one next test or data-review step.
  5. Propose one mitigation, limitation statement, or appropriate-use boundary.
Sentence frame: “One plausible cause is ___ because ___. This is not certain because ___. A next test would be ___. Until more is known, the system should/should not ___.”

AI-use disclosure

Do not use generative AI to draft the analysis. Use your own lab records or the provided case. Add a disclosure line.

Success criteria

  • I distinguish evidence from inference.
  • I trace error to more than one possible upstream cause.
  • I propose a testable next step and realistic boundary.
  • I avoid claiming the system is unbiased because evidence is limited.
