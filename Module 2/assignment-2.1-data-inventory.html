Data Inventory

Investigate how a familiar app or process turns representations into information and decisions.

Estimated time
15–20 minutes
Points
10
Submission
Canvas text entry or table upload
Format
One completed inventory

Task: Choose a familiar non-sensitive app or school process. Examples: a navigation app, music recommender, attendance process, library checkout, weather app, or online store. Do not use personal records or private data.

Required inventory fields

  1. System/process and purpose.
  2. Two likely observations or records.
  3. One label or category it may use.
  4. One claim it might produce or support.
  5. One decision a person or system might make.
  6. Two possible features or inputs.
  7. One important piece of missing context.
  8. One sentence explaining why data are not the same as complete reality.
Text template:

Purpose:
Observation 1 / Observation 2:

Missing context:

AI-use disclosure

Do not use generative AI to draft this work. You may use product documentation for factual details and cite it. Add: No AI assistance used or identify the tool and limited purpose.

Success criteria

  • I used a non-sensitive, appropriate example.
  • I correctly separate observations, labels, claims, and decisions.
  • I identify a plausible missing context.
  • I include a clear disclosure.

Rubric (10 points)

CriterionPointsFull-credit evidence
Accurate classification4Observations, labels/categories, claims, and decisions are distinct and plausible.
Features and missing context3Names useful inputs/features and a meaningful limitation.
Explanation and evidence2Explains incomplete representation; sources/inferences are clear.
Completion/disclosure1Readable, complete, and includes disclosure.
