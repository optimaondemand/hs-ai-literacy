Today’s Focus Question: How can choices made before training shape a model’s later errors?

Objectives

  • Identify sampling, labeling, measurement, proxy, and missing-data issues.
  • Trace an observed error to multiple plausible upstream causes.
  • Propose a cautious mitigation or boundary.

Materials: Reading 2.7 and Canvas text-entry access. No personal dataset is needed.

1 Retrieve and predict — 5 minutes

A plant classifier is trained mostly on sunny close-ups. Predict one likely failure condition and two possible upstream causes.

2 Experience: skewed-data exercise — 10 minutes

Use this fictional dataset: Category A has 90 clear daylight examples and 2 nighttime examples. Category B has 30 daylight examples and 25 nighttime examples. Labels were applied by three different volunteers using a short rule. The model frequently confuses Category A at night.

Identify: one sampling issue, one possible labeling issue, one possible measurement/context issue, and one next test.

Accessible text fallback: The dataset description above is the activity. Make a four-row table: possible cause, evidence, uncertainty, and next test.

Feedback check: Do not claim certainty about a cause you have not tested. A strong analysis says “one plausible cause is … because …; a next test would be ….”

3 Learn — 10 minutes

Read Reading 2.7. In notes, distinguish underrepresentation from label skew and define proxy. Add one reason a proxy can be useful yet dangerous.

4 Practice with feedback — 8 minutes

Complete Guided Practice 2.7 in Canvas. It includes scenario-to-cause matching and cautious mitigation choices.

5 Apply independently — 15 minutes

Open Assignment 2.7 — Error and Bias Analysis. Analyze either your lab/simulation results or a provided case. Identify evidence, plausible causes, a next test, and an appropriate boundary.

AI-use guidance: Do not use generative AI to write the analysis. You may use your own records and the lesson reading.

6 Build the portfolio — 5 minutes

Add the analysis to your portfolio. If your model did not show a clear skew, state what your small test cannot establish rather than claiming it has no bias.

Success checklist

  • I can name five upstream sources of bias/error.
  • I use evidence and uncertainty language.
  • I propose a test or boundary rather than a magic fix.
  • I submitted an individual analysis with disclosure.

AI Lab Notebook

Write 3–5 sentences: Which upstream source is hardest to notice in a small dataset, and why? Add disclosure.
