Identify possible environmental tests

Today’s Focus Question: Why does a perception system’s performance depend on the conditions under which it receives signals?

Objectives

  • Distinguish sensing from perception.
  • Compare image, audio, and other sensor-based inputs.
  • Predict how environmental changes can degrade a system.

Materials: Reading 2.2 and Canvas text-entry access. No live or external activity is required.

1 Retrieve and predict — 5 minutes

Write one reason a camera might identify an object in daylight but miss it at dusk. Then name a similar issue for speech recognition.

2 Experience: stress-test explorer — 10 minutes

For each system below, predict the consequence of one changed condition: (a) image recognizer—low light, blur, cropping; (b) speech recognizer—cafeteria noise, overlapping speakers, accent mismatch; (c) parking sensor—heavy rain, reflective surface, stale reading.

Accessible text fallback: Create a three-row table: system, changed condition, likely input change, likely output risk, and why. The table is the complete activity.

Feedback check: “It gets confused” is too vague. Explain how the signal or representation changes: fewer visible features, noisier sound, blocked object boundaries, or inaccurate distance data.

3 Learn — 10 minutes

Read Reading 2.2. In notes, define sensor, perception, occlusion, and distribution shift. Add one example of each from the experience.

4 Practice with feedback — 8 minutes

Complete Guided Practice 2.2 in Canvas. It asks you to identify likely stressors, explain signal changes, and choose a reasonable oversight response.

5 Apply independently — 15 minutes

Open Assignment 2.2 — Perception Stress-Test Memo. Describe three conditions that could degrade one AI perception system and explain why each matters.

AI-use guidance: Draft the memo yourself. You may cite documentation or a credible source for factual claims about the system.

6 Build the portfolio — 5 minutes

Add three environmental or input-variation tests to your future model investigation list. Avoid testing with people’s private images, voices, or identifying information.

Success checklist

  • I can distinguish a sensor signal from a perceptual interpretation.
  • I can name a concrete condition that changes the input.
  • I completed Guided Practice 2.2 and read feedback.
  • I submitted a memo with conditions, causes, and disclosure.

AI Lab Notebook

Write 3–5 sentences: Which stressor seems easiest to overlook when testing a perception system, and what would you do to test it fairly? Add your AI-use disclosure.
