Today’s Focus Question: What should an investigator record before claiming that a small model works?

Objectives

  • Train or inspect a small approved/simulated classifier.
  • Log categories, training examples, and variation.
  • Run and record first-round tests without hiding errors.

Materials: Experiment Plan, approved teacher tool or simulated-run worksheet, and Canvas submission access. No personal data or external account is required.

1 Prepare — 5 minutes

Reopen Assignment 2.4. Confirm that your task is bounded, non-sensitive, and has categories/outputs. If you cannot use the approved live tool, choose the simulated path below.

2 Choose your lab path — 5 minutes

Path A: approved no-code tool. Use only teacher-approved examples and settings. Do not upload identifiable people, voices, private records, or sensitive content.

Path B: simulated run. Use these categories: circle, triangle, square. Training log: 12 examples per class, mostly centered black shapes on white background. Test results: centered circle 1/1 correct; tilted triangle 1/1 correct; small square 0/1 predicted circle; triangle on patterned background 0/1 predicted square; partly cropped circle 0/1 predicted triangle. Treat these records as your first round.

Accessibility fallback: Path B is fully text-based and earns the same credit. You do not need an external training account.

3 Train/log — 15 minutes

Record category definitions, number/type of examples, and meaningful variation. If using the simulation, explain what the provided log includes and what it lacks.

4 Test — 15 minutes

Run at least 12 tests from your plan or complete 12 tests using the simulation pattern (normal, altered size, altered background, partial crop, ambiguous). Record expected result, actual result, correct/incorrect, and suspected cause.

5 Interpret — 10 minutes

Identify one pattern of success, one error pattern, and one revision you might try. Do not claim that a small test proves broad reliability.

6 Submit — 10 minutes

Open Assignment 2.5 — Training Data Log and First-Round Results. Submit your log and evidence record.

AI-use guidance: Do not use generative AI to fabricate examples, results, or analysis. You may use the approved tool or simulation and must disclose the path used.

Success checklist

  • I used a safe, bounded task or the provided simulation.
  • I logged training examples and variation.
  • I recorded 12 tests, including errors.
  • I identified a suspected cause and a revision idea.
  • I submitted a complete disclosure.

AI Lab Notebook

Record date/goal, tool or simulation path, categories, tests, what failed/surprised you, and what you would change next. Add disclosure.
