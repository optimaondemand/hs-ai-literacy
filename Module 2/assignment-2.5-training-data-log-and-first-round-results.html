Training Data Log and First-Round Results

Document what your small model/simulation learned from, how you tested it, and what happened.

Estimated time
20–30 minutes
Points
15
Submission
Canvas text entry, table, or file upload
Required
12 test records

Task: Submit evidence from the Lesson 2.5 lab. You may use an approved no-code tool or the teacher-provided simulated run. Accuracy is not graded as a contest; documentation and interpretation are.

Required sections

  1. Lab path used: approved tool or simulation.
  2. Task and category definitions.
  3. Training-data log: number/type of examples and variation included/missing.
  4. Evaluation table with at least 12 rows: test case, expected result, actual result, correct/incorrect, and notes.
  5. One success pattern.
  6. One error pattern and suspected cause.
  7. One proposed revision and one limitation/appropriate-use boundary.
Evaluation table headings: Test case | Expected | Actual | Correct? | Condition/variation | Notes or suspected cause.

AI-use disclosure

Do not use generative AI to invent logs, screenshots, outputs, or analysis. Identify the approved tool or simulation path you used. Add No generative AI assistance used or name any limited tool use honestly.

Success criteria

  • I record enough detail for another reader to understand the investigation.
  • I include 12 tests with variation.
  • I report errors honestly and propose a revision.
  • I state an appropriate-use limit and disclose my path.
