Learning-Approach Match

Match four problems to supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement, or transfer learning and justify each choice.

Estimated time
15–20 minutes
Points
12
Submission
Canvas text entry
Format
Four short analyses

Task: For each problem below, choose a reasonable first-fit learning approach. More than one method might be possible; your grade depends on clear reasoning and tradeoffs.

  1. Train a system to mark teacher-provided messages as “routine” or “needs prompt attention.”
  2. Explore anonymous, non-sensitive survey responses to see whether patterns or groups appear.
  3. Teach a simulated robot to navigate a maze using rewards for reaching a goal and penalties for collisions.
  4. Adapt an existing general image model to identify five teacher-provided local plant categories.

For each problem, include

  1. Your chosen approach.
  2. What information makes it fit.
  3. One limitation, risk, or question.
