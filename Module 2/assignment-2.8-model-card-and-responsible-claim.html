Model Card and Responsible Claim

Document your small model or simulated model so a reader can inspect its task, evidence, limits, and appropriate use.

Estimated time
20–25 minutes
Points
15
Submission
Canvas text entry or one-page document
Format
One-page model card

Task: Create a one-page model card for the approved small model or teacher-provided simulation you investigated. The goal is honest documentation, not a promotional summary.

Required model-card sections

  1. Model name and bounded purpose: What narrow task does it attempt?
  2. Inputs and outputs: What representations enter and what categories/results leave?
  3. Training context: What examples, categories, or simulation conditions were used?
  4. Evaluation evidence: What tests were run? Name at least one success and one error pattern.
  5. Limitations: Identify at least two conditions where evidence is weak or performance may degrade.
  6. Appropriate use: Name one limited, low-risk use with human oversight.
  7. Inappropriate use: Name one use this model should not support.
  8. Responsible claim: Write 2–3 sentences that state the task, evidence/conditions, and a limitation.
Responsible-claim frame: “In our [number/type] tests under [conditions], this model [observable task/result]. It showed [success/error pattern]. It should not be used to [inappropriate use] because [limitation or missing evidence].”

AI-use disclosure

Do not use generative AI to write the model card or claim. Use your own lab records or the provided simulation. Add: No generative AI assistance used, or name a tool and its specific limited purpose.

Success criteria

  • I state a narrow task, not a broad human-like ability.
  • I identify inputs, outputs, and training/evaluation context.
  • I report at least one success, one error, and two limits.
  • I distinguish appropriate from inappropriate use.
  • My claim is evidence-based and includes disclosure.

Rubric (15 points)

CriterionPointsFull-credit evidence
Purpose, inputs/outputs, training context4Task is bounded and system description is accurate.
Evaluation evidence4Specific tests, success, and error pattern are documented.
Limitations and use boundaries4Two meaningful limitations plus appropriate/inappropriate uses.
Responsible claim and disclosure3Claim is proportional to evidence and disclosure is complete.

  2. Use Text Entry or File Upload.
  3. Include every required section and your disclosure.
  4. Review the success criteria and submit.
