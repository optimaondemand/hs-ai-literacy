Perception Stress-Test Memo

Predict how changing conditions can affect an AI perception system.

Estimated time
15–20 minutes
Points
10
Submission
Canvas text entry
Length
250–350 words or equivalent table

Task: Choose one perception system: image/object recognition, speech recognition, face-unlocking, navigation sensing, or another appropriate system. Write a memo predicting three conditions likely to degrade performance.

For each of three conditions, include

  1. The changed condition (for example, backlighting, cropping, wind noise, overlapping speech, rain).
  2. How the signal/input changes.
  3. What output error or uncertainty could follow.
  4. Why the system may not have enough relevant evidence.
  5. One appropriate human oversight or testing response.
Text template:
System:
Condition 1: input change / likely risk / why / response
Condition 2: input change / likely risk / why / response
Condition 3: input change / likely risk / why / response
Conclusion: one sentence about limits of perception.

AI-use disclosure

Write the memo yourself. You may cite a credible source or product documentation for factual system details. Add a disclosure line naming any tool used and its limited purpose, or state No AI assistance used.

Success criteria

  • I identify three distinct, plausible conditions.
  • I explain changes to the signal, not only a vague error.
  • I connect each risk to oversight or a test response.
  • I include disclosure and any sources.

Rubric (10 points)

CriterionPointsFull-credit evidence
Stressors and signal reasoning4Three conditions and specific input changes are accurate.
Consequences and limits3Likely errors/uncertainties and reasons are clear.
Oversight/testing response2Each condition includes a sensible response.
Completion/disclosure1Readable, complete, disclosed, and sourced if needed.
